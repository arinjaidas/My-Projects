## Research approach

I make accounting and measurement conventions explicit, compare complex methods with transparent benchmarks, and distinguish descriptive fit from predictive or causal evidence. Collaborative research receives explicit credit.

**Core tools:** Python · pandas · NumPy · SciPy · statsmodels · Matplotlib  
**Additional tools:** R · Stata · MATLAB · Excel

Synthetic and illustrative examples are labelled. The payment-study repository also includes three recovered original scripts and eight historical aggregate result tables, with participant-level inputs excluded. The larger original BHARAT-FX and MAAKRO research bundles remain outside the public editions.
