# Provenance and attribution

## Historical context

GIPE Group 5 current-account sustainability work, with a forty-quarter descriptive assessment and a 105-quarter original forecasting rebuild.

## This public edition

New synthetic forecasting demonstration, distinct from the original MAAKRO code, data and reports.

Arinjai Das served as team lead and contributed to empirical work, ratios and reserves analysis, and project coordination. The original package separately passed 821 local numerical reproduction checks. The new public demonstration has its own code, data and checks.

## Scope of the evidence

The verification record concerns the public files and example described here. It does not establish a recovered historical trading record, a peer-reviewed publication, ownership of collaborators' work or independent reproduction of excluded archives. Synthetic examples are labelled at the source, metadata and results levels.

The original BHARAT-FX and MAAKRO archives and original payment-study scripts and results remain outside this public release. No original participant-level survey records or institutional microdata are distributed.
