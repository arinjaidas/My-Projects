# Original research scope and public implementation

The original collaborative work addresses India's current-account sustainability, financing composition, reserve transactions and external liabilities. It includes a forty-quarter descriptive assessment and a 105-quarter forecasting rebuild.

Arinjai Das served as team lead and contributed to empirical work, ratios and reserves analysis, and coordination. The prepared original numerical workflow was rerun locally and passed 821 numerical reproduction checks. These are separate from the ten checks attached to the new public demonstration.

## Original methodological scope

- Source and accounting audits, current-account components and financing reliability.
- Seasonal and autoregressive benchmarks, ridge VAR and component comparisons.
- Accounting-coherent forecasts, chronological evaluation and conditional scenarios.
- Benchmark safeguards and cautious treatment of provisional monthly bridges.

The archived evaluation uses the available data vintage retrospectively. It is not described as a real-time forecasting track record. Numerical reproduction establishes agreement with an implementation and its arithmetic checks, rather than proving predictive superiority or a causal policy relationship.

## Executable public scope

forecast_demo.py creates a separate 104-quarter synthetic example. It compares three methods, freezes a validation-based choice before a later test and reconciles component aggregates. Its code and generated outputs are available here. Original MAAKRO code, workbooks, reports, source inputs and derived research datasets remain outside this public release.
