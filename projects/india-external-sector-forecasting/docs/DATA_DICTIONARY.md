# Data dictionary

| Field or concept | Definition | Unit or rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| quarter | Consecutive quarterly period | At least 76 observations for the default full evaluation |
| goods, services | Net goods and net services balances | USD million; surplus positive |
| primary_income, secondary_income | Net income balances | USD million |
| fdi, portfolio, other_inflows | Net non-reserve financial inflows | USD million; inflow positive |
| derived aggregates | Current account, net inflows, reserve transactions | Calculated from components |

New synthetic forecasting demonstration, distinct from the original MAAKRO code, data and reports.

The default example is synthetic or illustrative. Real-data inputs require their own permissions, definitions and source audit. Missing or infinite values are not evidence of a valid measured zero.
