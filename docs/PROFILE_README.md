# Arinjai Das

I build reproducible analytical workflows for economic and financial questions. My interests span **applied econometrics, macroeconomics, household behaviour and financial risk**.

I am pursuing an **MA in Economics at the Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics, Pune**, with earlier economic-history research experience at the **University of Tübingen**. I hold a **BA (Hons.) in Economics from the University of Delhi**.

## Methods and tools

**Python** · pandas · NumPy · SciPy · statsmodels · Matplotlib  
**R** · **Stata** · **MATLAB** · **Excel**

My projects cover regression and discrete-choice models, time-series forecasting, macroeconomic accounting, option pricing, Monte Carlo simulation and sequence modelling. I make measurement conventions explicit, compare complex methods with transparent benchmarks, and distinguish descriptive results from predictive or causal evidence.

## Research you can reproduce

The portfolio contains **eight runnable analytical/model examples**, with **57 functional checks**, and **a complete narrative-review draft with figure reproduction**. The separate review repository links to the full paper and executable appendix in the portfolio hub.

Synthetic and illustrative data are labelled. Each project explains its provenance, contribution and limits, with credit for collaborative work and AI-assisted development. The larger original macroeconomic research bundles remain separate from the public examples.
