# Portfolio provenance

## Reconstructed and newly implemented code

Gold/equities, derivatives and the econometrics toolkit were reconstructed in October 2026 from documented earlier methods after their original local scripts could not be recovered. The ARMA and LSTM editions are also new public reconstructions. Historical drafted code is not treated as proof of a historical successful run.

The payment-choice workflow and the two macroeconomic examples were newly implemented on synthetic data. They are distinct from the original collaborative research archives. The public code and documentation were developed with ChatGPT assistance.

## Recovered written research

The September 2026 behavioural-recession manuscript is published as a complete Markdown narrative-review draft with its 35 references, six figures, four archived aggregate datasets and executable figure reproduction. Five empirical figures were redrawn from the archived appendix for legibility; the original conceptual figure is retained. This is a portfolio draft, with no new household survey or original econometric estimates.

## Attribution and excluded original materials

Project-specific credit appears in every README and provenance page. GIPE Group 8 contributors are named on the payment-choice project. The macroeconomic research is credited to GIPE Group 5. The econometrics project preserves the Tübingen research context under Prof. Jörg Baten.

Original BHARAT-FX and MAAKRO code/data/report archives and original payment-study scripts and historical results are outside this public release. No original participant-level survey records or institutional microdata are distributed.

## Scope of verification

The public release passed 57 functional checks, eight analytical/model example runs and one complete review-appendix reproduction. The review also checks two consumption-index calculations and the bounds of the Greek survey percentages; those assertions are separate from the 57 unit checks. The original external-sector archive's separate 821 local numerical checks do not count toward that total. MATLAB companions and the optional market downloader have not been executed for this release. A full independent literature and source-data audit of the review is not claimed.
