# Provenance and data

The gold/equities, derivatives and econometrics implementations were reconstructed in October 2026 from documented historical project methods because the original local source files were not recovered. They are labelled public reconstructions.

The payment-choice demonstration is also a new synthetic implementation. The original academic research scripts and historical aggregate estimates remain outside this release pending explicit public-sharing approval.

No original participant-level records or internship microdata are included. Bundled demonstration records and outputs are synthetic. Original research attribution is preserved on the project pages. The work involved iterative development and publication preparation with ChatGPT.
