# Portfolio provenance

## Public implementations

Gold/equities, derivatives and the econometrics toolkit were reconstructed in October 2026 from documented earlier methods after the original local scripts could not be recovered. The ARMA and LSTM editions are also public reconstructions. Drafted historical code is not treated as proof of a historical successful run.

The payment-choice workflow and two macroeconomic examples were independently implemented on synthetic data. They are distinct from the original collaborative empirical archives. The public code and documentation were developed with ChatGPT assistance.

## Recovered written work and scripts

The full September 2026 behavioural-recession manuscript is public in this hub, with 35 references, six figures, four archived aggregate datasets and figure reproduction. Five empirical figures were redrawn from the archived appendix for readability; the original conceptual figure was retained. This is a narrative-review research draft, not a peer-reviewed publication, new household survey or original causal estimation.

The separate payment-study repository publishes three original scripts and eight historical aggregate result tables. Those original empirical calculations have not been rerun for this release because participant-level inputs are excluded.

## Attribution and retained limits

GIPE Group 8 contributors are credited in the payment-choice repository. The macroeconomic work is credited to GIPE Group 5. The econometrics project preserves the earlier Tübingen research context under Prof. Jörg Baten. Project pages explain the portfolio owner's contribution and AI-assisted reconstruction.

Original BHARAT-FX and MAAKRO source/data/report bundles remain outside these public editions. No participant-level survey records or institutional microdata are distributed.

## Verification

The eight public analytical implementations passed 57 functional checks and complete example runs, both locally and in their separate GitHub Actions workflows. The full review appendix also reproduced successfully in the hosted portfolio workflow. Separate historical checks on held research are not added to this public check total. MATLAB companion execution and the optional market downloader are not claimed.
